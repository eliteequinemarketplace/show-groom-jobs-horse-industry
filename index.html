Elite Equine Marketplace connects show grooms with hunter jumper programs across the country — and connects trainers and barn managers with the experienced groom candidates their programs need. Show grooming is one of the most skilled and demanding careers in the equestrian industry, requiring extensive knowledge of horse care, competition preparation, braiding, and the specific demands of the hunter jumper show environment.

What Makes a Great Hunter Jumper Show Groom?

Hunter jumper show grooming requires a specific and highly developed skillset: expert braiding technique for both hunters and jumpers, thorough knowledge of competition preparation including bathing, clipping, and presentation, the ability to manage multiple horses' care simultaneously in a busy show environment, strong organizational skills for managing medication schedules, vet visits, and show entries, and the physical stamina to maintain high standards through long show days and weeks.

Circuit Groom Positions — WEF and Ocala

The Florida winter circuit creates significant demand for experienced circuit grooms — professionals who travel with programs to Wellington and Ocala for the January through April season. Circuit groom positions at top WEF and Ocala programs are competitive and sought-after, offering the opportunity to work with world-class horses alongside leading professionals in the sport.
